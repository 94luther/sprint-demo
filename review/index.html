What I am asking for. Attack it. Not encouragement, what is wrong.

Can any path take a payment twice, take the wrong amount, pay a merchant what they are not owed, or lose a thebe to rounding? Money is whole integers on purpose. Tell me where that breaks.

Two money models in here disagree by a quarter of every basket. One pays the merchant all of the goods and keeps only the delivery fee; a separate ledger splits the same order 75 / 18 / 7. Which should win?

And tell me what a passing test is defending that should not exist. A single note cash rule capped every order at 200 pula for weeks while thirteen tests passed over it.

One finding already, so nobody wastes time on it.

The rules layer is not wired into the order path. payments.ts, cash.ts, corporate.ts and liquor.ts are imported by nothing that serves a request. The tests prove the modules work. Nothing proves the app calls them. That is known and being fixed, so findings about it are not news.
